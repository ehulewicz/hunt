# Resume defense

Interview notes for `main.json`. One section per resume heading. These are the
places a sharp interviewer pushes, and the thing worth volunteering before they
find it. Keep this in sync when a bullet changes.

## Before any interview

- Know the provenance of every number on the page: what was measured, against
  what baseline, by whom. Every quantifier is an invitation to ask.
- Never let a gain that is relative to your own baseline sound relative to the
  state of the art. Say the comparison out loud.
- Three date ranges overlap (CPS runs through both research roles). Have the
  part-time/seasonal framing ready so it reads as normal student work.

## Education — Example University, BS CS, May 2028, 3.78

- May 2028 sets intern-level expectations. Lead with shipped work rather than
  titles, since the work is well ahead of the year.
- Have one course that visibly changed how you build, with an artifact attached
  rather than a grade.

## Middleware Engineer — Northwind

- "Led 4 Statements of Work" will draw *what does led mean here*. Be precise:
  scoping and coordination across teams, not people management. Pick one SOW you
  can walk end to end from customer conversation to production.
- 120+ tickets is a volume claim, not a difficulty claim. Pair it immediately
  with the single hardest ticket and what made it hard.
- The AJAX transition is the ownership story. Know what was risky — behavior
  parity, legacy edge cases, no downtime — and how you sequenced it.

## Technical Co-founder — Northhealth AI

- October 2025 – May 2026 is a closed range. Expect "what happened?" Have one
  non-defensive sentence, plus what you would do differently.
- Clinical trials and patient data raise compliance immediately. Know what data
  you actually touched, the HIPAA posture, and what you deliberately did not
  build.
- 22 organizations: how you reached them, and name a specific thing in the
  product that changed because of what they said.

## Undergraduate Researcher | Sample Lab — Example University Europe

- The 39% is fine-tuned versus unadapted MKDNet, **not** versus state of the
  art. YOLO beat your model on the same dataset. Say this before they ask.
- The non-reproduction is a strength when framed as method: 1 of 8 LaTOT parts
  reachable, same evaluation protocol, reported honestly. It was a data-access
  limit, not an accusation against the authors.
- Best thing to volunteer: NPR improved more than PR, which suggests MKDNet
  suits larger objects than the tiny ones it was designed for.
- Expect "you were in France?" Own it: Example University Europe in Metz, a joint lab
  with CNRS under Prof. Cédric Pradalier, which is also why the co-authors are
  French. It explains the term abroad without a detour.

## Undergraduate Researcher | Example Lab — Example University

- "Improving accuracy" is the weakest quantifier on the page. Have the real
  before/after, or a clean reason it is not shareable.
- Nuclear plus cybersecurity sounds sensitive. Be immediately clear it was
  simulated via ICSSIM, with no real plant data.
- Know what the anomalies actually were and why that model class fit them.

## Software Engineer Intern | Subcontractor — Example Public Schools

- 70% workflow reduction: how it was measured, against what baseline, and who
  validated it. This is the most checkable claim on the resume.
- May 2023 – December 2024 spans both research roles. Clarify the part-time and
  seasonal shape so concurrency reads as normal.

## AlphaResearch — UC Berkeley AI Hackathon

- "1000+ agents" and "500,000+ steps/sec" are the likeliest overclaim traps.
  Know exactly what was measured (aggregate across concurrent CPU-only
  sandboxes) versus what ran live at the event.
- Built with a team in a hackathon window. Be clear about what you personally
  owned — the distributed runtime — without diminishing the others.
- Why recursive spawning was the right design, and its failure modes: cost
  blowup, budget exhaustion, orphaned sub-agents.

## Personal Dynamics Engine

- The bullets are deliberately tensed: Building, Shipped, Designing. Know which
  is which. The modeling layer is designed, not trained — do not let enthusiasm
  promote it.
- 24/7 audio capture invites the consent question immediately. Have a real
  answer about audio from people who did not opt in.
- "Hundreds of records per run" comes from the jobs ingestion path, not the
  telemetry streams yet.
